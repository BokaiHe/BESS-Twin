# Renewable equipment explorers

Two Anatomy **Figure** illustrations, integrated into the existing equipment detail flow. They are local educational models, not manufacturer CAD or live telemetry. Open on the intact object; never auto-start a tour. Component names and explanations follow the app language (English in `src/i18n.tsx`, Chinese in `src/explorerCopyZh.ts`).

## Photovoltaic module

Concept: lift the protective laminate layers to reveal how a crystalline-silicon module packages and connects its cells; then inspect one representative cell. The drawing represents a glass/backsheet module, not every module technology. Thicknesses, cell count and spacing are illustrative. The inverter is downstream equipment, not inside this module.

Parts planned before geometry: four frame rails, four corner fasteners, two mounting rails, four clamps, backsheet, rear encapsulant, 24 representative silicon cells, interconnection ribbons, front encapsulant, glass, rear junction box, junction-box cover and two output connectors. Cell detail: rear contact, silicon base, emitter, anti-reflection coating, two busbars and twelve collection fingers. Assembly, laminate and cell detail are explicit viewing stages.

## Geared wind turbine

Concept: open a three-bladed upwind turbine's nacelle, then separate the mechanical conversion chain. This is a representative geared architecture; direct-drive designs omit the gearbox. The scene is an explanatory section, not the CAD of the plant's simulated 4 MW turbines.

Parts planned before geometry: foundation, tapered tower, two tower joints, access door, yaw bearing, bedplate, three housing panels, roof, roof hatch, rotor hub, three tapered blades, three blade-root collars, main shaft, two main bearings, two bearing pedestals, gearbox, gearbox cover, eight cover bolts, high-speed shaft, brake disc, generator, two generator feet, terminal box, six cooling fins, cooling unit, four louvers, wind-sensor mast and vane. Whole turbine, nacelle and separated drivetrain are explicit stages.

## Implementation and validation

Static geometry is generated by `scripts/build-renewables.mjs` with the installed Anatomy kit. Actual prisms and surfaces of revolution are recorded for geometry/depth audits. Animated SVG transforms and camera framing are updated with requestAnimationFrame through refs; React updates only on interaction. Motion is reversible, respects reduced-motion settings and stops when the page/figure is hidden. The background plant canvas pauses inside every equipment explorer.

Interaction tests cover explicit entry, idle first stage, direct object clicks, selection, reverse/reset, close/reopen and mobile/reduced motion. Existing BESS exploration remains independent.

## Geometry verification

The generated module has 94 physical parts, its cell detail has 18, and the turbine has 55. Each model passes the Anatomy clearance, support, depth-order and line-end checks. Geometry is sampled in 21 poses, including both assembly transitions. Round parts use 96 angular samples so they remain readable when the nacelle camera zooms in. No simulation capacities or equations are changed.

The PV cell view focuses a representative cell before separating its layers. The wind camera shows the complete rotor from the front quarter, then frames the nacelle. The shaft sections visible outside the bearing housings are separate drawing pieces; the hidden shaft sections are concealed by the housings.
